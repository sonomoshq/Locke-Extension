LOCKE EXTENSION · DATA SHARING

Choose what Locke can send to your desktop app

Locke checks requests you send on any website, on your device: the local Locke desktop app classifies each one, and screens the ones that look like an AI prompt or upload. It needs your permission before sending information outside your browser to that app.

Request data for local screening

For each request a page sends with a body — on any website, not only AI sites — the extension sends the request body (including prompts, chat messages and supported uploads), destination, path/query, page-set headers and provider identity through native messaging to the Locke desktop app. That app first decides whether the request is AI traffic; one that is not is allowed unchanged and not screened. AI requests are checked and get an allow, redact or block decision.

Depending on what you send, this can include personally identifying details, health information, financial and payment information, authentication information in page-set headers or content, personal communications, location, browsing activity, website content and search terms.

The extension does not send these copies to Sonomos servers, advertising services or analytics services. Allowed or redacted requests continue to the website you are using. The desktop app has its own local retention settings; pausing sharing cannot recall or delete data it already received. See the Locke privacy policy.

Optional connection indicators

These help the app show the extension's presence and confirm disabled-site settings. Screening and receiving your disabled-site settings still work when this is off. No prompt or upload content is sent through the presence indicator. In Microsoft Edge, automatic connector registration also uses these indicators; with this off, set up the connector in the desktop app if needed. Newer Firefox versions also ask for an optional technical-data permission.

What your choice means

Allow: Locke can send request data to the desktop app for screening. You can change your choices from the toolbar popup's Data sharing link.

Before you allow: leaving this page grants nothing. Pause data sharing stops further request-data and connection-indicator transfers to the desktop app. Leaving this page after allowing does not pause sharing. While Locke remains installed, requests it normally screens are held back; it does not block all browsing. To continue without Locke, choose Decline and uninstall, then reload affected tabs. You can also disable Locke in your browser's extension settings and reload those tabs.
